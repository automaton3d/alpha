# Aggregation integration review

Island identity is now represented explicitly by islandChief/shareChief:
K identifies itself by w; D refers to its chief through parent; S and P
are not members merely because their affinity or seeded leader field matches.
Existing body-body internal contacts and cohesion now use shareChief.
The old sameIsland helper was renamed sameAffinity to expose its real meaning.

The identity test verifies that different affinities may share a chief,
equal affinities do not merge chiefs, and a singleton's seeded leader is
not membership. Build/run: experiments/build_island_identity_test.bat.

The color-and-election variant is now implemented; see COLOR_CHIEF.md.
An additional experimental variant now adds reciprocal multi-axis attraction
through c; see COLOR_COHESION.md. It is not the older matched face-step
resolver, still reads reference center geometry, and has not demonstrated
spatial cohesion.

The subsequent COLOR_MATCHED variant exchanges movement proposals through W
and accepts only mutual partners. It removes additive movement overshoot,
and its six-frame test reaches an absorbing superposed geometry without P.
Superposition is admissible for this reduced resting system. The specific
inertial P x K/P x D experiment is now in INERTIA_MATCHED.md; electromagnetic
light-matter interactions remain excluded. No complete inertial mechanism
has yet been established for multiple propellers.
The reference election now accepts equal-charge sources across seed families
and affinities, using canElectChief. Affinity is preserved by this election.
The existing minimum-address leader comparison is unchanged; removing the
eligibility filter does not validate that comparison as the final physical rule.
The seed test measures chief populations, mixed-family groups, unresolved
parents, and distinct centers instead of requiring one chief per seed family.
Neither 9L chiefs nor L/3 constituents is a required test outcome.

Other inherited rules cannot simply be enabled: S x K writes reloc and
changes affinity through adoptLeader; same-parent D x D has no completed
orbital displacement rule; the separate reference cohesion resolver uses
source snapshots and source-center geometry. A translation into encounter,
c and unilateral draft updates is still required. No combined-system
island formation is claimed by the identity correction.
